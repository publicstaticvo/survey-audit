# SurveyAudit detector mapping

Names follow the ConTax table. Logic follows Appendix C, with original module names from the SurveyAudit checkout (`triage`). [Legacy execution and artifacts](../references/legacy-backend.md) explains how to reproduce a run. Several modules contribute to multiple categories. Use the row's predicate and trace to interpret a record rather than inferring a category from the module alone.

| ID and ConTax name | Operational criterion, inputs and decision | Original implementation and evidence | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 Internal Inconsistency | Extract coverage, comparison, synthesis, future-work and contribution commitments. Pair each with its complete intended scope and check FULFILLED / NOT_FULFILLED. Separately extract atomic paragraph notes (category, subject, predicate, object, quote), merge equivalent subjects and compare compatible notes for direct conflict under the same setting. | `consistent/contribution_consistent.py`, `consistent/note.py`, `consistent/internal_consistent.py` (10.1, 10.2, 11). Retain the declaration and inspected scope or both incompatible passages with their locations. | Paper Understanding, Scientific Verification |
| C2 Citation Hallucination | Decompose citation-bearing sentences into atomic facts with originating sentence and verify citations individually. Escalate inadequate title/abstract evidence to full text, chunked at up to 2,000 tokens with the five most relevant chunks reranked. Retain refutation or full-text-established lack of support. Missing access or ambiguous resolution leaves uncertainty. | `preprocess/citation_parser.py`, `preprocess/claim_segmentation.py`, `fact/fact_check*.py` (01, 06, 07). Keep manuscript proposition, citation identity, source spans and retrieval route. Aggregation also emits citation-resolution records. Inspect their separate identity evidence. | Literature Investigation, Scientific Verification |
| C3 Technical Inaccuracy | Verify substantive technical facts left after C2, excluding contribution, background and conclusion statements. Test manuscript-cited literature first. If inconclusive, query OpenAlex using non-stopwords drawn from the claim, rerank up to 200 papers and verify source passages as for C2. Retain explicit refutation. | `preprocess/claim_segmentation.py`, `fact/fact_check*.py` (06, 07). Keep originating claim, contradictory verbatim source evidence and retrieval trace. | Literature Investigation, Scientific Verification |
| C4 Missing Survey Scope or Selection | For systematic or structured coverage, inspect literature sources, search, eligibility, selection and equivalent methods. Appendix C's presence predicate is any eligible methods statement. Report one document-level omission when no such statement is identified. | `structure/` (12). Record the trigger and full inspected scope with the element sought. | Paper Understanding, Methodological Analysis |
| C5 Missing Contribution | Retrieve recent topic-relevant prior surveys, filter on titles and abstracts, extract each side's contributions and match them. Retain a fully subsumed target contribution with no explicit distinction or positioning. | `preprocess/get_reference_surveys.py`, `structure/` (03 and 12 are related routes). Keep evidence from both contributions and the paper scope checked for positioning. Use the artifact's actual route and settings. | Paper Understanding, Literature Investigation |
| C6 Missing Evidence | Screen uncited numeric, empirical, comparative, causal, theoretical or strong-generalization claims, including unresolved C3 candidates. Exclude cited sentences, figure/table content, routing, normative, future-work and concentrated discussion/conclusion passages. Check direct support in the smallest complete containing unit. | `structure/evidence_support_insufficient.py` (13 with 06/07). Keep the claim verbatim and bounded absence trace. Presence of support does not establish its adequacy or truth. | Scientific Verification, Methodological Analysis |
| C7 Missing Comparison | Enumerate sibling groups with at least two units and no comparison in joint scope, and single-unit subtrees with at least two topics, a research object and no comparison relation. Rank detected omissions by mean of normalized depth and citation-anchor signature gap, then remove subsumed opportunities. | `preprocess/paper_content_classify.py`, `structure/` (02, 12). Retain qualifying units, topics/objects and absence of comparison in the inspected scope. | Paper Understanding, Methodological Analysis |
| C8 Missing Cross-Study Synthesis | Enumerate siblings sharing a normalized topic/object key without a synthesis relation, plus topic-group subtrees with at least two topics and an object but no synthesis. Use C7 ranking and subsumption procedure. | `preprocess/paper_content_classify.py`, `structure/` (02, 12). Retain shared topics/objects and bounded absence of a higher-level relationship. | Paper Understanding, Methodological Analysis |
| C9 Missing Future Work | If no explicit future work, gaps, limitations or open problems occur anywhere, record one document-level omission. Otherwise screen up to ten representatives per subtopic for conflicting findings, evidence dimensions with systematic gaps, non-comparability, old concentration or a recent distinct paradigm. Rank by supporting sources, evidence dimensions and independent explicit gap signals. Verify ten proposals against up to three full texts each, check survey coverage, deduplicate and retain five. | `preprocess/paper_content_classify.py`, `structure/` (02, 12). Retain positive evidence of the gap and checked survey scope for NOT_COVERED. A low publication count alone has no gap predicate. | Literature Investigation, Critical Reasoning |
| C10 Missing Citation | For relevant prior surveys, check bibliography and explicit positioning. For named entities, merge aliases, query shared literature pool/OpenAlex and check up to three candidates against title/abstract evidence for origin, definition or establishment. | `scope/missing_papers.py`, `scope/uncited_entities.py` (05, 08 with 03). Keep the exact attribution target, source establishing it and inspected attribution scope. Unresolved entity origin leaves uncertainty. | Literature Investigation, Paper Understanding |
| C11 Missing Literature Coverage | For key works, build directed local citation graphs of anchors and first-order neighbors (cap 100), keep uncited papers with best local personalized PageRank at most ten, rank using citation sparsity, staleness and core coverage, diversify by marginal gains and verify topical relevance. For key subfields, run Leiden at 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and 8 on the uncited induced graph, remove small and duplicate communities, semantically verify coherence and substantive coverage versus stated exclusions, then rank using community size and relative cited/global PageRank. Appendix C describes five retained supports or instances per route. | `preprocess/literature_pool.py`, `scope/missing_papers.py`, `scope/topic_coverage.py` (04, 08, 09). Keep local scope and anchors, source passages or representative papers and ranking/verification trace. Supporting works can belong to one concern. Runtime settings can differ from manuscript defaults. | Literature Investigation, Critical Reasoning |

Appendix C expands several headings, including C4 Missing Survey Scope, C6 Missing Evidence Support, C9 Missing Future Work and Research Gaps, and C10 Missing Citation. Prompt aliases include Internal Contradiction, Literature Selection Missing, Novel Contribution Missing, Evidence Support Missing and Topic Coverage Missing. These names identify historical records. ## Checkout coverage

The inspected checkout's structural planner implements comparison, synthesis, document-level future work and document-level selection. It exposes the document-level branch of C9. C5's full prior-contribution subsumption procedure and C9's subtopic full-text gap verifier appear in Appendix C, with no corresponding executable path identified in this checkout. Module 03 supplies prior surveys and module 08 can check missing attribution under C10. C11 uses configurable retrieval and reporting settings. Interpret its traces with the settings actually used.
