# Reviewer concerns and ConTax

SurveyAudit builds ConTax from reviewer-stated concerns. Consistency concerns use closed text, cited literature, or independent literature. Existence concerns use context-dependent requirements or coverage discovery. Evaluation concerns address quality or adequacy of an existing element. The manuscript's taxonomy table and detector appendix are the authorities for operational definitions. Older baseline prompts use alternate labels and sometimes describe a broader or different test.

C12 External Norms and C13 Evaluative are historical, excluded categories. The manuscript table labels them Excluded. C12 covers norms and venue conventions. C13 covers open-ended judgments about adequacy or quality. Neither has an original detector. The new framework's structured synthesis is an independent capability.

Record reviewer concerns verbatim with provenance when supplied, or phrase new concerns as testable questions. Link the answer via a finding ID. Use the [detector mapping](legacy-mapping.md) for operational scope and the [working classification](underlying-failures.md) for the underlying defect.
