## Purpose

Identify which explanations and scope boundaries are justified by the evidence.

## Trigger Conditions

Use for extrapolation, competing interpretations, plausible counterexamples and important open questions.

## Required Inputs

Claim, operating conditions, underlying observations, and any suspected alternative or scope axis.

## Investigation Guidance

Identify the inference and what observations would distinguish it from a material alternative. Seek contrary primary evidence with appropriate scope. For universal claims, test examples against each stated prerequisite. Compare populations, settings, periods and interventions against the observed conditions. For research gaps or coverage, use the [legacy mapping](../../taxonomy/legacy-mapping.md) for C9 and C11 when artifacts are available. Track alternatives explored, what they explain and what would disambiguate them.

## Required Outputs

Return the [Skill result](../../schemas/skill-result.schema.json) and bounded alternatives, counterexamples or applicability judgments.

## Evidence Requirements

Anchor each proposed alternative in positive evidence or label it an open hypothesis. Record search scope and limits.

## Completion Criteria

Account for material alternatives and scope axes. Identify unresolved alternatives and consequential missing evidence.

## Escalation Conditions

Use [literature investigation](../literature-investigation/SKILL.md) for conflicting external evidence and [methodological analysis](../methodological-analysis/SKILL.md) to assess design explanations.

## Limitations

Bounded searches cannot rule out every alternative. Coverage decisions need their own cutoff and relevance rationale.
