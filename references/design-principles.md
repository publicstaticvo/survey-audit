# Design principles

1. Keep defects, audit duties and investigation capabilities independently extensible.
2. Design each check around a question and the evidence that can resolve it.
3. Preserve source versions, scope, original detector artifacts and evidence roles.
4. Allocate effort according to which results could change consequential judgments.
5. Record confirmed violations, tentative findings, unresolved questions and applicability explicitly.
6. Trace quality judgments to claims and contributions. Demonstrate a compensating relationship with evidence that addresses the same dependency or supports a separable contribution.
7. Treat generic procedures as portable. Establish domain coverage and assessment validity through separate empirical evaluations.
