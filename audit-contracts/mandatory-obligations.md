# Audit Contract

Instantiate all eight obligations for the requested audit. Record applicability, explicit targets, allowed outcome, reasoning, and evidence IDs for each. A partial audit reports exclusions as outside scope with a reason. For mixed target outcomes, retain individual target records and explain how they determine the obligation outcome. A substantiated violation takes precedence over an unresolved target, which in turn takes precedence over confirmation. Retain the remaining open targets even when a violation determines the overall outcome.

The proposition tested by each obligation is its stated requirement. **Verified** means evidence supports the requirement for the stated targets. **Contradicted** means evidence establishes a violation. **Unresolved** means the available evidence, access, or audit scope leaves a relevant question open. **Not Applicable** requires a positive explanation of why the obligation does not apply to the material or falls outside an explicitly requested partial audit. The following allowed outcomes apply independently to every obligation.

| Obligation | Purpose | Applicability | Required inputs | Required evidence | Completion criteria | Escalation conditions | Allowed outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reference Integrity | Verify existence, authorship, version and publication status of key references. | References material to audited claims or contributions. | Bibliography, linked claims and manuscript cutoff. | Authoritative metadata and version identifiers or an explicit search trace. | Resolve identity and status for every selected reference, or document attempts and open questions. | Conflicting metadata, ambiguous identity, inaccessible or revised source. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Citation Support | Test what a cited work supports. | Audited claims that attribute support to a reference. | Exact attributed proposition, marker and resolved version. | Manuscript passage and relevant primary-source passage under matching conditions. | Determine support, contradiction or insufficiency for each selected attribution. | Abstract-only access, multi-source attribution, version changes or scope mismatch. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Central Claim Verification | Establish traceability of consequential scientific claims. | Claims that materially support a contribution or conclusion in scope. | Claim inventory, qualifiers, dependencies and audit scope. | Primary grounds, assumptions and relevant counterevidence. | Resolve each selected claim or identify the missing link and its consequence. | Claim centrality, technical ambiguity, inconsistent results or multi-source reasoning. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Method-Evidence Alignment | Assess whether design and analysis can support the intended inference. | Claims that depend on an empirical, formal, computational or synthesis method. | Claim, method, data or corpus, reported analysis. | Protocol, proof, analysis or evaluation details and outcome locations. | Trace each claim through the method to observable grounds and evaluate decisive assumptions. | Confounding, questionable comparator, missing inputs or expertise needed to verify design. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Internal Consistency | Check commitments and recurring propositions. | Claims, definitions, figures, tables or commitments spanning manuscript locations. | Full relevant text and objects, with version. | Matched source passages or bounded search for a missing commitment. | Resolve each material discrepancy under shared conditions and identify unreadable regions. | Inconsistent notation, missing appendix, apparent discrepancy across versions. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Counterevidence Awareness | Investigate material contrary evidence and alternative explanations. | Audited conclusions with plausible competing accounts or contrary evidence. | Claim, assumptions, domain and date boundaries. | Primary contrary or competing evidence and a trace of searches with inclusion decisions. | Evaluate the strongest relevant alternatives and describe residual gaps. | Credible conflicting studies, sensitivity to plausible assumptions, unclear applicability. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Scope Verification | Test the limits of claims and review coverage. | Claims with scope, transfer or representativeness implications. | Claimed boundary, sampled settings and relevant exclusions. | Original qualifiers, observed settings and evidence of transfer or omitted in-scope literature where relevant. | Compare each material scope axis and explain unsupported extensions. | Boundary ambiguity, important omitted strata or cross-setting extrapolation. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |
| Uncertainty Reporting | Expose unresolved, qualified and conflicting judgments. | Every audit. | All target outcomes, source access, limitations and open questions. | Linked uncertainty records and the conditions required to resolve them. | Report each material limitation, its affected targets, and actionable next evidence. | Conflicts left unexplained, missing source coverage, or downstream conclusions stronger than their grounds. | Verified, Contradicted, Unresolved, Not Applicable |

## Autonomous investigation

Select paths, searches, tools, Skills and depth based on the paper, risk, and what would change the conclusion. Prioritize dependencies of central claims and increase depth for strong conflicts. Stop when the evidence meets the criteria or when remaining access, expertise, or scope limits are recorded. Consult the [evidence requirements](evidence-requirements.md).
