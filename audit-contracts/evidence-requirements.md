# Evidence requirements

Maintain stable evidence IDs with source identity, version, locator, content and evidential role. Use the original PDF or canonical source to verify extracted passages and read neighboring qualifiers. Label abstracts, metadata, artifacts, and full text at their actual granularity. For unpublished inputs use a local source identifier. Record the manuscript and literature cutoff separately from the date of investigation.

- **Passage** records verbatim material, its page or section, and what it establishes.
- **Artifact** records an inspectable table, dataset, calculation, or metadata record with a locator and observation. Identify which expression, cell, row, version, or field was checked.
- **Bounded absence** records scope, search criterion, coverage, and observations. Limit absence conclusions to the inspected scope.
- **Search trace** records the service, query, filters, date, and results or access failure. A search trace establishes the extent of investigation.

Separate independent sources from copies, versions and derivative summaries. For reused evidence verify that source version, scope, target, and role still fit. Keep contrary evidence attached. A tentative finding needs positive evidence and a specific uncertainty. An unresolved issue can record the question and missing evidence without asserting a defect. Give a reason for each non-applicable check. Rate confidence by directness, completeness and agreement of evidence. Express limited access and conflicting sources explicitly.
